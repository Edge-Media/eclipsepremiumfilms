Our Services

Solar Film

Solar films (nano-ceramic/sputter/magnetron types) with heat-repellent/absorbing technology and UV rejection, so as to improve comfort, protect interiors and save energy.

Privacy Film

Privacy films such as one-way mirror films, frosted or etched films, and tinted films—that are commonly used for bedrooms, offices, medical facilities to increase security and privacy protection.

Safety Film

Safety films are designed to hold shattered glass together, preventing injuries from sharp edges and flying debris. They’re often used in toilets and offices to reduce the risk of injury from accidents or break-ins.

Decorative Film

Decorative films are used to enhance interior aesthetics/ branding while providing added privacy. Available in frosted, etched, and patterned styles, they’re used in commercial spaces to transform glass surfaces without blocking natural light.
